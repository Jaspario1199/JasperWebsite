Gas-to-Electric Tractor Conversion

Personal ProjectIndependentSide ProjectAustin, TX

A favor for my old robotics teacher that turned into a full drivetrain swap: take a worn-out gas tractor, pull the engine, and rebuild it as a fully electric machine.

Teardown

First step was a complete disassembly of the gas drivetrain — engine out, fuel system removed, and the transmission evaluated for what could be reused versus replaced.

Electric conversion

With the chassis stripped, I welded in a new electric transaxle and replaced the engine bay with a battery system — making the tractor fully electric with instant torque and zero fuel maintenance.

This one was finished before I thought to document it properly — apologies for the sparse photos.
