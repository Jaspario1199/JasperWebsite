VEX U Championship Robots with Team WHOOP5

Design & Fabrication MemberAggie Robotics · Team WHOOP5Aug 2023 – May 2024College Station, TX

In VEX U's OVER UNDER game, robots score by controlling tri-ball game objects — and the best teams can retain, launch, or push them on demand. I contributed to Team WHOOP5's robots through AutoCAD schematic redesign and iterative mechanism development across a full competition season.

The extendable friction launcher

My main contribution was iterative development of an extendable friction launcher — a single mechanism that could retain a tri-ball for control, launch it across the field, or push it through contested zones as the match demanded. Versatility in one mechanism meant fewer subsystems, less weight, and faster cycle times.

Results

WHOOP5 took 1st place at the 2024 VEX U OVER UNDER Texas State Championship and went on to place at the World Championship.
