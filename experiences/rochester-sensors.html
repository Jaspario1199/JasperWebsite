Rochester Sensors Internship

Mechanical Engineering Intern  |  Rochester Sensors (Amphenol)  |  May 2026 – Present  |  Dallas, TX

Rochester Sensors, an Amphenol subsidiary, builds liquid-level and pressure sensing systems for industrial and vehicle applications. As a mechanical engineering intern I get to work across the whole product lifecycle: CAM programming on the machine floor, FEA before physical testing, and environmental qualification of finished assemblies.

CNC Machining and CAM

I design and optimize CNC toolpaths and machining workflows in CamWorks. The highlight so far has been a tooling redesign on a milled component that cut cycle time by 18% while still meeting every GD&T requirement on the drawing.

Environmental Qualification Testing

These sensors spend their lives in fuel tanks and on vehicles, so I run the tests that prove they will survive: thermal cycling, salt spray ingress, and vibration testing to validate long-term field reliability before assemblies ship.

Design Troubleshooting and FEA

On active-development products I diagnose and resolve design and manufacturing defects in SolidWorks to keep production on schedule. I also run FEA stress and thermal analyses in SolidWorks Simulation ahead of physical testing, which catches problems while they are still cheap to fix.
