Sensor Manufacturing Engineering at Rochester Sensors

Mechanical Engineering InternRochester Sensors (Amphenol Subsidiary)May 2026 – PresentDallas, TX

Rochester Sensors builds liquid-level and pressure sensing systems for demanding industrial and vehicle applications. As a mechanical engineering intern I work across the full product lifecycle — CAM programming on the machine floor, FEA ahead of physical testing, and environmental qualification of finished assemblies.

CNC machining & CAM optimization

I design and optimize CNC toolpaths and machining workflows in CamWorks. My favorite win so far: a tooling redesign on a milled component that cut cycle time by 18% while holding every GD&T requirement on the drawing — pure throughput gained with zero quality traded away.

Environmental qualification testing

Sensors live hard lives in the field, so I run the tests that prove they'll survive: thermal cycling, salt-spray ingress, and vibration testing to validate long-term reliability of sensor assemblies before they ship.

Design troubleshooting & FEA

On active-development products I diagnose and resolve design and manufacturing defects in SolidWorks, preventing production delays. I also run FEA stress and thermal analyses in SolidWorks Simulation ahead of physical testing — catching problems in simulation is dramatically cheaper than catching them on the test bench.
