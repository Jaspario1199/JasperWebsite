Soccer Shots — A Spring-Powered Toy Prototype

Design ProjectTexas A&M UniversityJan 2025 – Apr 2025College Station, TX

Soccer Shots is a multiplayer tabletop toy: a spring-powered launcher fires the ball while an opposing player works a goalie mechanism to block the shot. I took it from concept sketches to a functional 3D-printed prototype.

Design

The full assembly was modeled in SolidWorks: a spring-powered ball launcher with an adjustable-tension plunger, and a goalie mechanism that slides across the goal mouth. Designing for 3D printing meant respecting printable overhangs, snap-fit tolerances, and minimal support material from the start.

Prototype

The prototype was manufactured via 3D printing and basic assembly methods — springs, fasteners, and press-fits — producing a playable proof of concept.
