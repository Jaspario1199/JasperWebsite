Automatic RFID Bike Lock

Personal Project  |  Independent  |  Dec 2025 – Present  |  College Station, TX

Fumbling with a key or combination every time you park a bike is friction that doesn't need to exist. This project is an automatic lock: tap an RFID fob and a solenoid-driven mechanism retracts the lock, with LED and audio feedback confirming the state.

How It Works

An Arduino Nano reads the RFID scanner and drives solenoids for the locking mechanism, with LEDs and audio feedback for state confirmation. The enclosure is a custom SolidWorks design, 3D-printed with an integrated display window.

Status

The prototype authenticates, actuates, and reports state reliably. Current work is on the retraction drive and weatherproofing the enclosure for daily outdoor use.
