Automatic Retractable RFID Bike Lock

Personal ProjectIndependentDec 2025 – PresentCollege Station, TX

Fumbling with a key or combination every time you park a bike is friction that shouldn't exist. This project is an automatic lock: tap an RFID fob and a solenoid-driven mechanism retracts the lock, with LED and audio feedback confirming the state — no keys, no dials.

How it works

An Arduino Nano reads the RFID scanner and drives solenoids for the locking mechanism, with LEDs and audio feedback for state confirmation. The enclosure is a custom SolidWorks design, 3D-printed with an integrated display window.

Status & next steps

The prototype authenticates, actuates, and reports state reliably. Current development focuses on the retraction drive and weatherproofing the enclosure for daily outdoor use.
