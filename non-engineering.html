On campus

Campus involvement

A few of the organizations I give my time to at Texas A&M.

One Army

One Army is a Texas A&M men's organization built on brotherhood, leadership, and service.

Still Creek Ranch

One Army's philanthropy is Still Creek Ranch, a Bryan, Texas home that shelters and mentors boys who have been abused or neglected. I help mentor the boys and support the fundraising that keeps the ranch running, an effort One Army has backed with more than $900,000, largely through Gladiator Dash, our obstacle-course mud run.

Special Events Logistics Chair

Separate from the philanthropy side, I serve as One Army's Special Events Logistics Chair, a role I held through my junior year and am carrying into my senior year. I lead 80+ members and manage a $30K+ budget to design and build the organization's large-scale event structures, from first mockups and sketches through construction and on-site logistics.

Aggies Create

Aggies Create is the student organization our whole ECOFIL team came out of. It is an official 501(c)(3) that runs as a startup incubator, funding student ventures like ours, and its other branches consult with companies.
